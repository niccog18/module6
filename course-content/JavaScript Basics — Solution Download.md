# JavaScript Basics — Solution Download

**GitHub:** `module-06-web-streamlit/solutions/exercises/flashcard-app/`

Compare your solution to the reference. Key things to check:

- Does clicking "Show Answer" reveal the answer, and clicking "Next" hide it again?
- Does the progress indicator update correctly ("Card X of 5")?
- Does the app loop back to Card 1 after the last card?
- Did you use `addEventListener` rather than inline `onclick` attributes?

Your styling and card content will differ. Different approaches to showing/hiding the answer (display property, textContent clearing, classList toggling) are all valid.
